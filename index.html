KINDNESS & SAFETY

Friendly, respectful, and safe.

Good conversations should respect everyone's comfort, privacy, and boundaries.

Respect comes first

No pressure, harassment, bullying, or inappropriate messages. Anyone can end a conversation whenever they wish.

Protect your privacy

Don't share passwords, PINs, home addresses, school details, private documents, or financial information in chats.

Know the limits

Online companionship cannot replace trusted people in your life or professional help when you need it.
